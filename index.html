FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the service — a per-role search fee, a monthly sourcing/lead gen retainer, or a fixed project fee for B2B Lead Lists, Reverse Recruitment, and CV Formatting & Optimization. You receive exact pricing in your written scope before any work begins.

Who will I actually be working with?

You work directly with me (Marium) from your initial brief through final shortlisting and delivery — your project is never passed between departments or junior coordinators.

What if a hire does not work out?

Eligible recruitment placements include a 30-day replacement warranty, with full replacement terms clearly outlined in your written scope.

How long does a search, lead sprint, or CV formatting take?

Most scored shortlists are delivered within 2 to 3 weeks of agreeing on the scope, verified B2B lead lists within 5 to 10 business days, and CV Formatting within 3 to 5 business days.

How do you find candidates and B2B leads?

Beyond active networks, I use advanced LinkedIn Recruiter, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Boolean, and X-ray search techniques to identify passive talent and verified decision-makers who are not reachable through generic databases. Every candidate is vetted against a custom scorecard, and every lead list is qualified against your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP).

Is my search confidential?

Yes. Your initial conversation is strictly confidential, and I regularly conduct quiet searches without disclosing your company name until candidates are vetted and approved by you.

Can individuals hire Recruitexa for Reverse Recruitment or CV Formatting?

Yes. Both Reverse Recruitment and CV Formatting & Optimization are open to individual professionals targeting US, UAE, UK, or global remote roles.
